# GOC and IUPAC reference atlas — 10 November 2026

The separate **GOC & IUPAC orders** module has Effects & orders, IUPAC priority, and Active practice tabs. NCERT and Guided Learning provide entry points to the same canonical module and dataset.

## Content

- 31 scoped comparisons and decision sequences: inductive/mesomeric effects, hyperconjugation, intermediate and alkene stability, acidity, basicity, nucleophilicity, substitution, acyl reactivity, aromatic effects, CIP and traditional electromeric notation.
- 21 attachment-specific substituent classifications; 18 common/extended characteristic-group classes; an interactive seniority helper; 15 worked molecular examples.
- 22 explanation-backed MCQs, including 12 Master questions, and accessible order-building practice. Revealed answers do not earn independent credit.
- Three Master electron-flow events: Michael addition, E1cB collapse and addition in a qualified stepwise SNAr model.

Every comparison states conditions or a reference framework. Unlike-group +M/−M rankings are not a universal physical constant. The Hammett mini-lab uses net para substituent constants for benzoic-acid ionization in water at 25 °C; model reaction sensitivity is separately labelled.

## References and scope

The source register links IUPAC Blue Book P-41–P-43, P-51/P-59 and P-92; the IUPAC physical-organic glossary and Gold Book; NCERT; primary university resources; and the OpenStax publisher. Edition-independent textbook page numbers were not invented.

The chart covers ordinary neutral classes with special/cyclic cases identified. Alcohols and phenols share the current hydroxy class. Characteristic-group seniority is distinct from CIP and from reaction reactivity. The helper compares supplied classes; it does not infer a complete name from an arbitrary molecular graph.

These rewritten study notes and selected examples have source mapping and automated checks. They are not independently expert-certified. A complete specialist audit of all previously existing app chemistry is not claimed.
