# Connected mechanism sheets: scientific review scope

This update renders existing curated mechanisms as a complete numbered map, with enlarged before/after diagrams available on the same page. Product reminders, transition-state illustrations and selectivity checks are separated from chemical events; they are not labelled as additional intermediates.

## Validation and limits

Tests check complete route coverage, graph references, carbon/heteroatom valence limits, the Aldol atom/charge balance, reagent-specific templates, and separation of concerted events from explanatory views. These checks detect classes of mistakes; they are not proof of universal mechanistic correctness. Existing notes were reviewed for internal consistency. This is not an independent expert audit of every arrow and every condition.

Metal, heterogeneous-surface and reagent-dependent transformations retain explicit schematic/condensed scope labels. Omitted counterions, ligands, solvent proton relays and condition-dependent alternatives are not represented as fully balanced elementary reactions. Before using the app as a publication or authoritative answer key, have a chemistry subject specialist independently review the full collection. No blanket zero-error certification is claimed.
